# Stage 6 — Basic Stats + Live Awareness + Standings Repair

Planned as one complete functional increment.

- Add a lightweight Basic Stats destination to team pages when a usable team-leaders feed is returned.

- Repair MLB standings division grouping with MLB StatsAPI as a demonstrated MLB-only fallback; retain the existing ESPN standings path for the other leagues.
- Preserve Stage 5 roster grouping, customization, navigation, schedule, retry/cache behavior, and all current team artwork.
- No Full Stats table, News, weather, attendance analysis, or additional cosmetic team themes in this stage.
